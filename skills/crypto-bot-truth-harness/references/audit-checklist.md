# Judgment checklist

Use this after the mechanical profile.

- TH-01: Can every percentage be reconstructed from displayed numerator and
  denominator? For model claims, is base rate/lift disclosed?
- TH-02: Is the conclusion driven by realized watchlist-top capture and unified
  portfolio outcomes rather than teacher, shadow, or training metrics?
- TH-03/04: Were features available before the label, and is the score from an
  untouched chronological holdout?
- TH-05: Are compared windows equal and complete? Are downtime and partial days
  excluded rather than silently scored?
- TH-06: Does replay match the live candidate population and maximum available
  period? If not, label it provisional.
- TH-07: Is rollback immediate? Are downside, false positives, exits, capacity,
  and portfolio risk guarded?
- TH-08: Was a rejected result preserved with period, N, metrics, and verdict?
- TH-09: Do MD, config, code, and current runtime agree?
- TH-10: Are freshness, coverage, denominator size, and unknowns in the verdict?
- TH-11: Is profitability portfolio-level after costs against a named baseline?
- TH-12: Are spec, tests, replay evidence, staged scope, commit, and push present?
